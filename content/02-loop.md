No autopilot, no arcade physics. Awake to a crippled reactor. Learn and adapt quickly, or fail and get forgotten forever.

## Budget the power
A crippled reactor, two buses, eighteen systems that all want to be on. The grid sheds by
priority, not by fairness. Handle a realistic power grid to power what really is important, at the moment.

## Plan the burn
Real Newtonian-Kepler physics on a solar map drawn to scale. Nothing solves the transfer for you.
You aim, burn and you must live with your calculations, bad or good. Scavenge for resources, explore stations and derelicts.

## Sleep through it
Transits take weeks. Put the crew back in the pod and set what will wake you. Realistic pilot aging, the solar system does not stop while you sleep.

## Fly the last kilometre
Close quarters happen in manual flight. Hand-fly the approach, match the station's spin, hold seven
gates until the clamp takes.
